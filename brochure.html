The palette is drawn from the place: the understory greens outside the windows, the quilt blues of the region's textile tradition, and the house's own fieldstone chimney, whose grey names the bedroom-wing colours. One warm white carries the connected great room and loft; each room beyond gets a single colour, used once or twice, all grayed to the same volume. Outside, the entry doors go deep green while the cedar siding, railings, garage doors, wood trim, wood ceilings and the stone itself stay as they are. The half bath also stays as-is.

Working notes

The white. Whites are decided in the room, not on screen. Brush a sample quart of Alabaster on the great-room gable and one north-facing wall; if it reads yellow in morning light, BM White Dove is the fallback. Do this before ordering the 11 gallons.

The deck and porch floors. The floors carry solid paint today; the siding and rails carry semi-solid stain and are not touched. Opaque over opaque is a normal recoat: wash, scrape anything loose, scuff-sand the gloss, spot-prime bare wood, then two coats of an acrylic solid deck stain labelled for previously painted surfaces, or a porch-and-floor enamel, in the walnut tone. If the existing paint might be oil-based, test a square foot and tape-pull it after a few days before committing the six gallons. Going back to a see-through stain would mean stripping to bare wood; that door is closed and was not wanted. The small front porch (≈ 72 sf) can join the walnut for about half a gallon more.

The doors. Steel and fibreglass doors get washed, deglossed and a bonding primer before the enamel. One green gallon covers the front, side and porch doors with spare for touch-up. The garage doors and the half bath stay exactly as they are — owner decision, 1 Sep 2026.

Halls and stairs. Not separately discussed during the colour rounds; the takeoff assumes they carry Alabaster with the field. Strike the 560 sf if they stay as they are (−3 gal).

The basement. The finish rooms on the S-1–S-14 set inherit from this scheme when they price: new bedrooms follow Revere Pewter, den and flex follow Alabaster. Their quantities belong to the S-11 paint sheet, measured, not estimated.
