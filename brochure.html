Working notes

Checks to make before ordering or painting.

The white

SW 7008 Alabaster is approved for the hallway. Sample it there in daylight before ordering. The 11-gallon figure covers the earlier full scheme, including great room and loft, not just current sale work.

Deck and porch floors

Dark Walnut solid stain is approved for the deck; manufacturer and product are not selected. Existing floors carry solid paint. Confirm product compatibility, coverage and prep instructions before ordering. Earlier porch-floor allowance remains provisional. A return to transparent stain would require stripping to bare wood.

Doors

SW 6208 Pewter Green is approved for front/back and garage doors. Wash, degloss and use compatible bonding primer on steel and fibreglass. Measure the full door scope and confirm enamel coverage before ordering; the earlier one-gallon allowance did not include garage doors.

Halls and lower level

Hallway repainting in SW 7008 Alabaster is approved. Earlier halls-and-stairs allowance is 560 sf and about 3 gallons; verify the hallway’s actual area. Basement colours are outside this palette update; basement quantities belong to measured sheet S-11.
