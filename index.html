The house

Built in 2008, log-sided over a stone-skirted foundation, with red oak floors and trim throughout. Two bedrooms, two full baths and a half bath, a loft over the great room, and a walk-out basement with a drawn and priced two-bedroom finish plan that conveys with the house. The great room rises two stories to a glass gable; a wood stove sits in the fieldstone chimney that runs from grade to the ridge. The interior's whites stay as they are; the refresh repaints the kitchen and bathrooms, the doors and the outdoor floors.

Fixtures & finishes

Hardware finish, decided: antiqued brass. The house's doors already carry antiqued brass knobs and hinges, so the cabinet pulls and knobs join a finish the house has worn since 2008 — warm against the golden oak, and of a piece with the historic paint colours. Plumbing finish is the one open question: brushed nickel with the appliances, or Delta's champagne bronze to sit with the brass — both are compared on the sale-prep page. Polished nickel was considered and passed over: a third, shinier metal against the brass the doors already have.
Kitchen: high-arc pull-down, brushed nickel — Delta Leland class, representative style
Baths: widespread two-handle, brushed nickel — Delta Woodhurst class, four sinks
Cabinet hardware: antiqued brass bar pulls and knobs, ~44 pieces
