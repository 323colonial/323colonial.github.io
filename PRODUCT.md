## Users

Prospective buyers are primary users. Realtors and the owner use the site as supporting audiences when answering property questions and arranging showings.

## Product purpose

Present a photo-led narrative for weekend and second-home buyers: understand the house through seven exact paragraphs and coordinated photographs, explore all 65 images, and contact the listing brokerage. The owner-directed layout uses house colors, a full-width hero, fixed address/contact header and docking listing facts.

## Operating context and boundaries

Static HTML/CSS and small progressive-enhancement `gallery.js` / `listing.js` / homepage-only `seasonal-hero.js` (the seasonal runtime for hero and narrative, reading `assets/seasons-next/frames.json`); no frontend framework or build/runtime service. `analytics.mjs` adds gated PostHog analytics using a pinned self-hosted SDK; `docs/analytics.md` records browser policy, event definitions, account setup and verification boundaries. Native image and gallery links work without JavaScript. `listing.js` measures the header/facts and enables sticky photo sequences only when content fits; it respects reduced motion and preserves focused photo links. At viewport heights of 500px or less, facts scroll normally; at widths of 800px or less the contact header also scrolls normally to preserve reading space. Only nearby sequences preload their next photo, and the closed viewer image is lazy-loaded. Both buyer pages share `listing.css`. Their informational analytics notice collapses after 10 visible-tab seconds, remembers dismissal for 30 days, and keeps permanent Privacy and opt-out access before the footer (colonial-6ma). Focus/hover cancels dismissal; reduced motion skips animation; no-JS and blocked storage retain the notice. See `docs/analytics.md` and `tests/privacy-notice.html`; tracking authorization is unchanged.

Resize reading position (colonial-9r4): changing viewport size changes all earlier narrative heights, the current photo step and responsive sticky offsets; native scroll anchoring alone lost several photos in reproduced cases. `listing.js` caches the settled reading point and makes one instant correction after the existing coalesced resize rebuild. Pinned sequences retain section and fractional photo progress, including the last-photo hold; ordinary content uses a visible block and approximate height fraction. Gallery reflow retains the first visible figure in document order. Short-height/reduced-motion inline photos remain available; crossing into inline layout keeps the dominant photo, not its blend fraction. Text rewrap is approximate, page bounds clamp, and desktop paragraph/photo pairing cannot preserve an exact mobile text line. Focused controls and open dialogs keep native scrolling/focus priority; fragment navigation, print entry and input during a pending resize cancel correction. No scroll interception, animation, native-anchor disabling, persistent state or visual-viewport-only handling is added. Tests cover layout-viewport changes and native print-media geometry in Chromium, not physical-device rotation, browser-chrome/keyboard behavior, pinch zoom or every engine.
