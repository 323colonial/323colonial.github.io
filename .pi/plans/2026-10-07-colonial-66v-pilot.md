## Timing

Frames follow the hero's 12 steps, where the year also runs through one day: late-summer midday, fall evening, winter blue hour, dawn at snowmelt, spring morning. `.pi/artifacts/seasons-pilot/pilot.py` holds the date and time assigned to each step and computes sun azimuth and elevation, then tells the model whether direct sun can reach the wall or glass in view.

## Method

1. Every request edits the unchanged master derivative. No output is ever edited again.
2. Reference image 2 is the approved hero frame for the same step.
3. Anchors first: steps 0, 3, 6, 9. Owner review. Then fill the other eight steps with the two approved neighboring frames of the same photo added as references.
4. Model output is 2K and is resized to the master's exact pixel size. No alignment correction or measurement has been done yet.

Prompt rewrite, `prompt2.py` (exterior and outlook photos). One rule: what is listed under CHANGE changes, everything else stays. Former "exceptions" (plants and stands, the original's baked shadows) are now ordinary CHANGE lines, so nothing contradicts KEEP.
